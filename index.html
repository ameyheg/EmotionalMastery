Module 02

From Destruction to Direction

Anger is an evolutionary signal, but a terrible master. Discover how to harvest the energy of anger while neutralizing its destructive radius.

Box Breathing

4-4-4-4 counting methodology used by Navy SEALs to manually override the autonomic nervous system and halt adrenaline.

  • 1. Inhale deeply for 4 seconds.
  • 2. Hold the air in your lungs for 4 seconds.
  • 3. Exhale smoothly for 4 seconds.
  • 4. Hold your lungs empty for 4 seconds.
  • Repeat cycle 4 times to reset the vagus nerve.

Cognitive Restructuring

The ABCDE model to catch distorted thoughts (catastrophizing) before they mutate into uncontrollable emotional states.

  • Activating Event: What triggered you?
  • Belief: What did you tell yourself?
  • Consequence: How did you react?
  • Dispute: Is that belief 100% true?
  • Effect: Replace with rational thought.

The 6-Second Pause

The exact window required for the prefrontal cortex to process emotion, preventing the infamous Amygdala Hijack.

Emotion travels to the emotional center (amygdala) faster than the logic center (prefrontal cortex).

  1. Feel the trigger spike.
  2. Stop all physical movement.
  3. Count slowly to six.
  4. Respond, do not react.
